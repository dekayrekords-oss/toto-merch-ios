# The Meow manual

## Meow's place

Meow lives in three rooms. The office is in the middle, the living room is to the left and the bedroom is to the right. On an iPad or in landscape you see all three. On an iPhone you see one room at a time, and the view follows Meow as it wanders. Tap the room names at the top to look next door; Meow comes along.

Tap Meow to pet it. Tap the bed, the food tray, the litter box, the couch or a cat tower and Meow goes there. These are only for fun: there are no hunger or care meters.

## The office

- **Computer:** Meow's work — every reminder, draft, shortcut and screen answer, with what actually happened.
- **Corkboard:** your pinned North Star and its next step.
- **Sticky note:** what's next.
- **Letter tray:** people waiting on you.
- **Jar:** money things coming up.
- **Lamp:** lights up when Meow is keeping an idea for you.
- **Clock:** opens the Clock app.
- **Drawers:** your iPhone apps (top) and Meow's tools (bottom).
- **Ask Meow:** type a question without leaving the room.

## The living room

- **Stereo:** the song playing in Music, with play, pause and next. It needs Media & Apple Music access; other apps keep their songs private on iOS.
- **Big-screen TV:** opens YouTube.
- **Bookshelf:** Books and Audible, and this manual.

## The bedroom

- **Big window:** the weather.
- **Old TV and console:** opens Delta, a game emulator from the App Store. You need your own game files.

## North Stars

In Flow, tap **Adjust → North Stars**, or tap the North Star line below the date. Add a long-term goal, why it matters, and the steps that make it possible. Meow connects relevant Flow items and things you tell it to your goals. A connection means "this may matter"; it is not proof that you accomplished something.

## Comments

Every Flow item has **Comment**. A comment is a note for Meow, never a message to anyone. On a calendar item, “this event is canceled” cancels just that one occurrence, after checking.

## Ask about a screen

In Shortcuts, pair **Take Screenshot** with **Ask Meow about this screen**, then put it on Back Tap or the Action Button. Meow reads the picture on the phone and helps; it can't tap or change anything in the other app.

## Quiet

Say or type “be quiet” and Meow stops talking until you speak to it again.
